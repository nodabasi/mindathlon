Testing Guide

Try Mindathlon before everyone else

Mindathlon isn't public on Google Play yet. It won't come up in search — it is visible only to the accounts on the testing list. If your email address has been added, the three steps below get you installed.

Not sure whether you're on the list, or want to be? odabasi.developer@gmail.com — I add addresses to it by hand.

It takes about five minutes, and the one real condition is that the app stays installed on your phone for 14 days.

One rule first: don't tap the addresses below. Press Copy, then open Chrome and paste into the address bar.

Here's why: when you tap one of these addresses, Android hands the page to the Play Store app, which can't render it and shows a blank white screen instead. Pasting into the address bar skips that handoff and the page opens normally.

Three steps

  • Check which account you're on

    Open the Play Store and tap your profile picture in the top right. The Gmail address shown there has to be exactly the one you gave me and that went onto the testing list.

    If it's different, switch accounts or tell me and I'll add that address instead. If your phone has more than one Google account, this is the mistake people make.

  • Become a tester

    Open a new tab in Chrome. Copy this address and again paste it into the address bar:

    https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.odabasi.brainathlon

    Tap Become a tester on the page that opens. When you see "You're a tester", this step is done.

    If you still get a blank screen, the Play Store is still intercepting the address. Turn that off:

    1. Settings → Apps → Google Play Store
    2. Open by default
    3. Turn off Open supported links
    4. Go back to Chrome and paste the address again
  • Install the app

    Once you're a tester, the store page opens for you. Copy this address and paste it into the address bar:

    https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.odabasi.brainathlon

    Press Install. If the button isn't there, wait 5–10 minutes and refresh — it takes Play a little while to register your access.

If you get stuck

It says "App not available"

Two possible reasons: you're on the wrong Google account — switch from the top right of the page — or that address isn't on the testing list yet. If it's the second, email me and I'll add it; it opens up within a few minutes.

The page is still completely blank

Apply the yellow box in step two. If that doesn't help, update Android System WebView and Chrome from the Play Store, then go to Settings → Apps → Google Play Store → Storage → Clear cache.

Searching the Play Store doesn't find it

That's expected. Apps in closed testing never show up in search; they open only from the address in step three.

Nothing works on my phone

Do step two in a browser on a computer, signed in to the same account. That leaves only the install to do on the phone.

Why 14 days

Google requires at least 12 people to stay in a closed test for 14 days before an app from a personal developer account can go public. That's why it matters that you don't uninstall early — doing so resets the count for you.

You don't have to play. But if you do, that's where the real value is: send me your feedback.
