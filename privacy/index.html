Privacy Policy

Mindathlon collects no personal data

Effective 1 September 2026 · Applies to the Mindathlon mobile app for iOS and Android

The short version

Mindathlon does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. The app has no account system, no servers of its own and no internet connection. Everything it saves stays on your device.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your settings — the interface language you picked.
  • Your scores — for each finished marathon, the difficulty and the total time.

This information is written to your device's local app storage. It is not sent anywhere, it is not backed up to us, and we cannot see it. You can erase the scores at any time from Scores → Clear scores in the app, and deleting the app removes all of it permanently.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-up, e-mail address or login
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location
  • No network requests — the app works entirely offline

Sharing a result

When you finish a marathon you can tap Share. This opens your device's own share sheet with a short line of text containing the difficulty and your total time. Nothing is sent until you choose an app to send it to, and where it goes from there is governed by that app's privacy policy, not ours.

App stores

Mindathlon is distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play. Apple and Google collect their own download and usage statistics as part of running those stores. That collection happens on their side, under their privacy policies, and we receive only anonymous aggregate reports — never information that identifies you.

Children

Mindathlon is suitable for all ages and is not directed at any particular age group. Because the app collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Your rights

Data-protection laws such as the GDPR and Turkey's KVKK give you rights to access, correct and delete your personal data. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to hand over or erase. The data on your device is entirely under your control and you can delete it as described above.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what it stores or transmits, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date at the top will change.
