Questions, bugs and feedback

Write to odabasi.developer@gmail.com and you will get an answer within a few days.

To help us reproduce a problem, please include your device model, your iOS or Android version, and which stage you were on.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Mindathlon has no sign-up and no login. Open the app and play.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, completely. The app never connects to a server.

Where are my scores stored?

Only on your device. Finished marathons are saved with their difficulty and total time, and shown under Scores in the main menu.

How do I delete my scores?

Open Scores from the main menu and tap Clear scores. Deleting the app also removes everything it stored.

My scores disappeared after reinstalling. Can I get them back?

No, and this is by design: nothing is uploaded anywhere, so there is no copy to restore from. Uninstalling removes the app's local data permanently.

How do I change the language?

Main menu → Settings → Language. English and Turkish are supported.

What is the boost bar?

Each stage has a par time. Finishing under par charges the bar — the further under, the more charge. You can spend that charge on hints later in the run.

Can I quit a marathon in the middle?

Yes, but the run will not be saved: your time and progress are lost, and the app asks you to confirm before quitting.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

Neither. The app is free and complete as it is.
